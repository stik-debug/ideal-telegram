GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Can I try a lesson before paying?+

Yes. Courses can include a free preview lesson. Open a course to see which lessons are available before enrolment.

How much do courses cost?+

Each course displays its price in Kenyan shillings on the course card and course page. Check the current listing before making a payment.

Do I need experience to start?+

Start with a beginner-friendly course and follow the lessons in order. Each course description explains what you will learn.

Can I study on my phone?+

The website is designed to adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop screens. A stable internet connection is recommended.

How do payments work?+

Payment options shown by the platform depend on what has been configured by the academy. Do not pay outside the official checkout or share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone.

Will I get a certificate or a job?+

Certificate features depend on course completion and platform availability. Completing a course does not guarantee employment, income or a job placement.
