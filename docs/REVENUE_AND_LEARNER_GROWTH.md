# Revenue and learner growth plan

## Revenue streams to build and validate
- Free introductory lessons that demonstrate quality.
- One-time paid courses and transparent course bundles.
- Guided bootcamps with clearly stated mentor hours and feedback deliverables.
- Optional CV, portfolio and project-review sessions.
- Team training seats for schools, community groups and businesses.
- Employer-sponsored projects or job listings only under transparent terms.

## Payment requirements
Use a configured provider and server-verified callbacks. Do not unlock paid content from a browser-only success message. Handle duplicate callbacks idempotently, store references, issue receipts, provide refund/support policy, and test failed, pending, successful and reversed transactions. Keep secrets out of source control.

## Growth loop
1. Publish useful free lessons and a real project preview.
2. Ask learners what they want to achieve and recommend a focused path.
3. Help learners finish an artifact and share it voluntarily.
4. Request honest feedback after meaningful milestones; do not reward only positive reviews.
5. Offer a referral reward only after a qualifying, verified event and publish the terms.
6. Track acquisition source, activation, completion, repeat purchase, refunds and learner satisfaction.
7. Improve courses based on assessment results and learner feedback.

## Owner metrics (real records only)
Track sign-ups, first lesson started, first lesson completed, course completion, assessment pass rate, portfolio artifacts submitted, paid conversion, refunds, net revenue, repeat purchases, referral conversion, applications tracked, interviews reported and employment outcomes verified with learner consent. Distinguish self-reported outcomes from independently verified placements. Never present empty metrics as zero-success claims or invent values for marketing.

## Required safeguards
No job guarantee, no fabricated vacancies, no fake reviews, no hidden recurring charges, no unconfigured payment method presented as live, and no referral payout before eligibility is verified. Clearly disclose that course completion alone does not guarantee work or income.
